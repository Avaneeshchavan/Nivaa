# Nivaa quickstart for the clinic

1. Open **https://nivaa-kohl.vercel.app** and sign in with your approved clinic email and password. Use **Sign out** when finished on a shared device.
2. On **Patients**, type a name, phone, past diagnosis or medicine. Tap a result to open the record. The header's **Find patient** also works, with Cmd/Ctrl+K on a keyboard.
3. For a new patient, choose **Add patient**, enter details and record allergies. A possible-duplicate warning links to the existing record. Review it before deciding to save another patient.
4. Open the patient and choose **New prescription**. Review the allergy banner. Enter diagnosis, add medicines or select a clinic-reviewed template. Edit dosage, frequency, duration and advice for this patient. Choose **Ayurvedic** in the medicine-system selector when needed. You can add a missing medicine through custom entry.
5. Optional visit measurements are under **Visit measurements**. Typed advice goes under **Notes and advice**. Draw in **Handwritten notes**, or choose **Attach paper note** to upload a JPG/PNG/WebP photo. Wait for the upload to finish before saving.
6. Edits to a new prescription save to a cloud draft after a short pause. Keep the page open if draft saving reports an error. Reopen the same prescription-entry route to restore its draft. Check the patient before continuing.
7. Use **Show print preview** when you want to inspect the printout. Choose A5 or A4, then **Save Rx** or **Save and print**. Handwriting and paper photos appear on additional pages. Allow the print tab if the browser prompts.
8. For an older visit, choose **View Rx** to print, **Duplicate as new Rx** to prepare today's repeat prescription, or **Add / edit notes** to update advice and handwriting. Saved-note editing requires **Save notes**. A changed-in-another-tab message means you should review the newer record before trying again.
9. In **Templates**, choose **New template** or an existing template's edit button. Enter its title/diagnosis, add medicines, adjust **Edit details**, and **Save template**. The editor body scrolls while Save/Cancel stays visible. Have the clinic doctors review the inherited common templates before use.
10. In **Settings**, update doctor/clinic details, upload the clinic logo and private signature, and save the profile. Logos are public branding. Put patient photos in the handwritten-note area.

Before routine clinic use, check both iPad orientations, the on-screen keyboard, finger/Pencil drawing, opening/printing A4 and A5, draft restoration and both doctor accounts. During an outage, use the clinic's paper process and reconcile records when service returns. The owner handles backups and recovery.
